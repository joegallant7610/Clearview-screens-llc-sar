Choosing the Right Screen Material

The best screen depends on how you use your outdoor space, the insect protection you need and the durability you want.

  • 18/14 fiberglass screen: A common choice for pool cages and lanais.
  • 20/20 no-see-um screen: Finer mesh designed to help keep smaller insects out.
  • Super Screen: A durable screening option for customers seeking longer-lasting performance.
  • Pet-resistant screen: Useful for selected lower panels and areas exposed to pets.

We can discuss the practical differences and recommend options for your enclosure and budget.

Repair a Few Panels or Rescreen the Whole Cage?

Not every damaged pool enclosure needs a complete rescreen. The right choice depends on the age and condition of the existing screening.

Individual Panel Repair

Often suitable when most of the enclosure screening is still in good condition.

Partial Rescreening

A practical option when damage is concentrated on one side or section.

Full Rescreening

Worth considering when many panels are brittle, deteriorating or failing.
